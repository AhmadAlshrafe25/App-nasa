---
name: SpaceHealth visual identity
description: User-stated visual rules for the SpaceHealth dashboard.
---

SpaceHealth uses one consistent charcoal/near-black and dark-navy palette with neon cyan accents across its screens, including the holographic anatomy, Astro, and sign-in. Use off-white text and muted gray-green supporting text. Reserve green, yellow, red, and gray for semantic health/sensor statuses, not decorative differences. Use square corners throughout and put Arabic Typesetting first in the font stack, with Arabic-capable system fallbacks. Prefer larger, readable text over dense layouts, especially for alerts and wrist screens. Preserve Space and Sky as distinct dark themes with the same cyan accent and understated astronomical details.

**Why:** After the holographic anatomy was added, the user asked to make the entire color palette the same. This supersedes the earlier mixed cyan/lilac accent direction.

**How to apply:** Keep page surfaces, controls, selected states, charts, dialogs, and app-owned authentication styling in the same dark/cyan palette. Keep alert meaning clear through labels and icons as well as semantic color, and let text wrap on narrow displays rather than shrinking it.
